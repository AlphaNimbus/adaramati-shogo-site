02 / The Art Practice

The work
speaks.

A multidisciplinary practice exploring material, identity, perception, memory and the human condition.

I work across painting, mixed media, oil, 3D/sculpture and metalwork. The studio is a place to experiment with material and form — to make questions tangible, and allow an idea to become an object with its own presence.
