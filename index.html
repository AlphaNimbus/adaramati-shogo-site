The Art Practice

01 / Artist

Material, memory, vulnerability and strength.

My art practice explores the human condition through material and form. I work across painting, mixed media, oil, 3D/sculpture and metalwork, often combining objects and human forms with reflective surfaces.

The work is tactile, contemporary and deliberately interdisciplinary — using process as a way to ask questions about identity, perception, culture and what it means to be human.

Art is where I question.
Creative direction is where I translate.

Two practices. One way of thinking.

Whether I am making an artwork or directing a brand experience, I begin with the same instinct: understand the idea deeply, find the tension inside it, and give it a form people can see, feel and remember.

This is the value of a multidisciplinary practice. It allows strategy, visual language, culture and technology to inform one another rather than operate as separate disciplines.

About

03 / Perspective

A creative practice built between studio and strategy.

I am Adáramátì Shògo — a Creative Director and multidisciplinary artist working across art, design, culture and technology.

My practice moves between making and directing: developing ideas, shaping narratives, building visual systems and creating experiences that can hold attention in a crowded world.

I am particularly interested in the space where cultural meaning meets contemporary technology — and in using creative work not simply to decorate an idea, but to give it presence, clarity and consequence.
