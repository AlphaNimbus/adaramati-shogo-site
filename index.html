About

03 / 04

Adáramátì Shògo is a multidisciplinary artist working across painting, sculpture, and emerging technology.

Independent studies in painting and sculpture, alongside a B.A. in History and International Studies from Adeleke University, Osun State, Nigeria (2020).

Most commissions are executed through his creative studio, Alpha & Nimbus Studio, in collaboration with marketing agencies for global brands — integrating art and technology across brand activations.

Founder of Ramati Art Africa, organizing competitions and exhibitions to support young artists across the continent.
