Two practices.
One point of view.

Adáramátì Shògo is a Creative Director and multidisciplinary artist working across art, culture, identity, technology and experience.

The person
behind the work.

I move between making and directing: developing ideas, shaping narratives, building visual systems and creating experiences that can hold attention.

My multidisciplinary practice allows strategy, visual language, cultural thinking and technology to inform one another — while keeping the integrity of each discipline.

Most commissions are executed through my creative studio, Alpha & Nimbus, in collaboration with marketing agencies for global brands. I'm also the founder of Ramati Art Africa, organizing competitions and exhibitions to support young artists across the continent.
